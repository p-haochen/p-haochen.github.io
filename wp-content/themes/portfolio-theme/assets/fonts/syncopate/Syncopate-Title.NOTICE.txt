Syncopate-Title.woff2 is a modified subset of Syncopate-Regular.woff2.
Created for the PO HAO CHEN homepage title on 2026-09-29 with fontTools
4.66.0, retaining only the characters in "PO HAO CHEN" and their OpenType
layout dependencies. The original Syncopate-Regular.woff2 remains unchanged.
